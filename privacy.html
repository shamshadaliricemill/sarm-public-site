Privacy Policy

This policy describes the private S.A.R.M — Shamshad Ali Rice Mill Android application and this informational website.

Purpose and access

S.A.R.M is a private single-user rice mill recordkeeping application. Access is limited to the authorised owner. It has no public registration, social sign-in for app access, advertising, analytics or data-selling features.

Information used by the app

The app handles the owner’s Firebase identity and session, and business information entered by the owner: dates, receipts, party names and phone numbers, products, weights, bag counts, vehicle numbers, expenses, monetary amounts, payments and notes. Financial periods, seasons, settings, derived totals, outstanding balances and lightweight audit metadata support the records.

Firebase Authentication manages email/password authentication. The app does not manually persist passwords. Google Cloud Firestore stores business records and associated metadata under owner-only access rules. Business dates are separate from server audit timestamps.

Device storage and biometrics

Local preferences include appearance and biometric opt-in, and non-secret backup settings and timestamps. Temporary exports, recovery files and SDK-managed session/cache data may exist on the device. Android verifies biometrics; S.A.R.M does not collect or store fingerprint images, templates or biometric data. Biometrics unlock an existing Firebase session rather than create one.

Google Drive data and permission

Google connection is optional and separate from Firebase login. The app uses https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file only, for app-created backup files and the visible S.A.R.M Backups folder. It does not request full-Drive access or scan unrelated personal files. Google account email is used to verify the intended backup account; file identifiers and metadata support history and integrity checks.

Archives contain business/master/settings data needed for recovery. They exclude passwords, OAuth credentials, biometric data and signing keys. The Google SDK manages authorisation; the app does not place tokens or secrets in its backup archives. OAuth tokens are used to authorise Google API requests.

Manual backup is deliberate. Automatic backup is OFF by default and runs only at eligible foreground opportunities when enabled. Verified backups are retained up to the latest 30 per owner; older eligible app backups move to Drive Trash after a new verified backup. Interrupted uploads may leave pending files that are not valid recovery candidates. Recovery requires archive checks and explicit confirmation and is restricted to an empty business database or the same interrupted recovery.

Use, sharing and service providers

Data is used for private recordkeeping, authentication, summaries, reports and chosen backup/recovery operations. Firebase/Google process information necessary to provide authentication, database and Drive services. Data is not sold, used for advertising or sent to an analytics service by S.A.R.M. User-chosen exports may be shared or printed through Android; the owner controls the selected recipient or destination.

S.A.R.M’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and choices

Business records remain until the owner deletes them. Permanent deletion removes the authoritative transaction and reverses its derived contributions; a lightweight deletion audit may remain. Deleting a live transaction does not retroactively remove it from older backups or exported reports. The owner should manage those copies separately. Google normally removes files from Drive Trash after 30 days.

The owner can disable automatic backups, disconnect Google in the app, revoke access through Google account settings and manage backup copies in Drive. Revoking access does not itself delete existing backups. Contact us for questions about stored records or deletion; this policy does not promise an unimplemented automatic account-deletion feature.

This website

This static site has no login, forms, analytics scripts, advertising, cookies added by S.A.R.M or embedded remote trackers. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. Emailing support shares the information you choose to send through your email provider.

Security and contact

Owner-only rules, authentication, optional app unlock and backup integrity checks reduce risk; no system guarantees absolute security. Backups are not described as end-to-end encrypted. Protect the device, account and exported files. Policy updates will appear here with a revised date.
