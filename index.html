S.A.R.M — Shamshad Ali Rice Mill is a private, single-user Android application for recording and reviewing the mill’s purchases, expenses and sales.

How your data is handled
S.A.R.MSHAMSHAD ALI RICE MILL

Designed for the owner’s daily records

BUSINESS RECORDS

Purchases, expenses & sales

Paddy purchases, mill and Sareo expenses, electricity bills, bank markup and rice, Daro, Chunno, rice husk and paddy sales. Financial periods, parties and optional seasons keep records organised.

REVIEW & EXPORT

Reports and statements

Search and filter records, review summaries and outstanding balances, and export PDF or CSV reports. Android sharing and printing are available through the device’s native interfaces.

PRIVATE ACCESS

Firebase sign-in & app unlock

Firebase Authentication manages the existing owner’s email/password session. Optional Android biometrics protect access to that session. There is no public registration or guest access.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE CONNECTION

A secondary backup, under your control.

Cloud Firestore is the primary database. Optional Google Drive backup and recovery use only the drive.file permission to manage app-created backups in a visible S.A.R.M Backups folder. This does not grant unrestricted access to your entire Drive.

Backup Now is user initiated. Automatic backups default to OFF and, if enabled, run opportunistically while the authenticated app is open. Recovery requires validation and explicit confirmation. The app retains the latest 30 verified backups; eligible older app backups move to Drive Trash.

Google Drive release acceptance is still pending; connection availability depends on the current Google OAuth consent configuration. This website is informational, not a web login, public service or download portal.
