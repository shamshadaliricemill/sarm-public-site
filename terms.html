Authorised use

The app is intended for the authorised rice mill owner, not public registration or a consumer subscription service. Use it only for records you are authorised to manage. Do not share access credentials, bypass security or enter data unlawfully. This informational website provides no web-based access to business records.

Records and calculations

The owner is responsible for accurate entries, financial-period selection, reconciliation and review of calculations, reports and exports. The app provides recordkeeping tools, not professional accounting, legal, tax or investment advice. Do not rely on an unreviewed report as a substitute for checking source records.

Deletion and recovery

Deleting a business entry is permanent in the primary database and requires confirmation. Derived balances and summaries are reversed; minimal audit history may remain. Previously exported or backed-up copies are separate and are not erased by transaction deletion.

Drive is an optional secondary backup, not the primary database. Validate backup status and keep suitable independent copies. Automatic backup is OFF by default and is opportunistic, not guaranteed background scheduling. Retention keeps the latest 30 verified backups and moves eligible older app files to Trash. Recovery requires integrity checks and explicit confirmation; it is not an unrestricted merge into existing records.

Google OAuth release readiness and consent configuration can affect backup access. No uninterrupted availability, guaranteed recovery or error-free operation is promised.

Privacy and security
